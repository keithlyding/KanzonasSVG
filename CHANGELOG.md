| 1.0.4 | 2026-11-13 | (this one) | The speed budget for drawing an SVG set to 4 times what it takes (0.45 s): 0.15 s had been set before the test picture was made detailed (its SVG 22.6 MB: 0.11 s on 4 cores), and a slow build machine took 0.17 s, so 1.0.2 wasn't published on its own; its changes came out in 1.0.3, and its notes are in this release's. |
| 1.0.3 | 2026-11-13 | 13e2e53 | Speed: a big picture (over 3 megapixels) shows a quick preview (a 1-megapixel copy traced, about 0.1 s) while the whole picture is traced (a 12-megapixel photo: first SVG at 1.3 s, was 2.9 s; another number of colors: first result at 0.29 s); the smoothed picture and its color histogram are kept (another number of colors: 0.9 s -> 0.55 s); pixels put into colors on all cores, the histogram on all cores; Auto settings tries its color counts in parallel (0.44 s -> 0.26 s, the same choice); the engine's caches hold two pictures (the preview's copy and the picture). |
| 1.0.2 | 2026-11-13 | 20b0738 | Windows: the color reduction counted in 32-bit numbers (Inkscape's unsigned long is 32 bits on Windows) that overflowed on big pictures, so detailed pictures got worse colors than on Linux (The Starry Night with Auto settings: 4.58% badly wrong pixels on Windows, 2.87% on Linux); now 64-bit, and colors of equal brightness are stacked in the same order on every platform. Found by the new quality test on its first Windows build. Fixes from a battery of tests to break it (tests/engine_battery.sh with AddressSanitizer and UBSan, tests/fuzz_engine.sh with libFuzzer, tests/battery_app.py): a crash on quitting after Copy SVG; SVGs of thousands of small shapes shown only in part (Qt reads at most ~32,000 points per path: paths now split between outline groups, and the viewer reads its own SVGs whole); NaN or infinite settings (--threshold nan) undefined behavior in the engine; near-copies of colors with Smooth on clean pictures (palette colors that end up nearly the same are merged); Enlarge first could build a picture of hundreds of megapixels (now at most 40 MP); traces could make SVGs of gigabytes (now stop at 500 MB with a message); Auto traced with another threshold than the panel showed. The engine battery runs (with the sanitizers, a new seed each build) and a short app battery run in every build. Speed: the window never freezes (it froze 0.3-0.9 s on photos: Qt's SVG renderer holds Python's lock while it draws; the engine now draws its own SVGs, core/src/drawing.cpp, through ctypes, 8-20 times faster and matching Qt's drawing, in a background thread per side; picture files are read in the background; the checkerboard is one fill); tracing uses all cores (color layers and brightness steps in parallel, smoothing and edge snapping by rows; the same SVG on 1 to 8 threads; ThreadSanitizer clean): a 12-megapixel photo 1.9 s -> 1.0 s; the reduced colors are kept, so changing speckles, corners, optimize, tolerance, stack or background traces again in 0.33 s instead of 1.0 s; speed budgets in tests/test_speed.py. Testing rules as in KanzonasPDF and KanzonasCAD (AGENTS.md): every change runs the unit tests (with short batteries, speed budgets and tracing quality on public-domain reference pictures against tests/reference/quality.json); a major revision also runs tools/full_battery.py (long sanitized, ThreadSanitizer, fuzzer and app batteries, threads, drawing against Qt, big pictures, window speed) and is used by hand. |
