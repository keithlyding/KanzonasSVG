| 1.1.1 | 2026-11-13 | a601fd4 | Auto settings sets every setting (speckles, smooth corners, optimize, tolerance, Autotrace's settings and Enlarge first back to the values that suit the picture; before, they stayed as they were). Line art (a plan, a drawing or a screenshot of one: a plain background with thin lines, at least 30% of what's drawn in runs at most 4 pixels across or down): no smoothing (it wiped thin lines out; their soft edges aren't noise), Enlarge first 4 times (lines a pixel or two wide trace as solid shapes), and the colors counted on the lines' middle pixels too (lines have no flat areas: a plan of gray lines and blue labels got 2 colors and lost its lines; now 9, every line kept). A thin color is only dropped as an edge shade when it touches both colors it lies between: a one-pixel gray line on white was dropped as a shade between white and a dark area elsewhere (a toolbar in a screenshot). A floor plan traced with Auto: before, 4 colors, smoothed, its gray lines (chairs, table, door) gone; now 9 colors, enlarged 4 times, all there (0.9 s, 2.3 MB). The reference pictures give the same numbers (none is line art). tests/test_auto_line_art.py (the gray-line test fails on the engine before: 6 of 20 points kept). Auto settings on flat pictures with more than 16 colors (illustrations, maps, charts, screenshots): Auto tried at most 16 colors, so such a picture was taken for a photo and got 14 or 15 (a 19-color illustration got 8: its sun lost its yellow, its door turned purple). Now, when a clean picture's flat areas are covered by 17 to 64 colors (each at least 1/2000 of them), Auto tries up to that many and keeps every one of them (flat pictures of 20, 24, 32, 40 and 48 colors: picked 14, 15, 14, 15, 15 before; now each gets its colors, all exact in the SVG but slivers of mostly covered shapes, 0.1-0.2% of the picture). Photos, paintings and gradients are picked for as before: tools/quality.py's reference pictures give the same numbers, and Auto takes no longer on them. tests/test_auto_many_colors.py (fails on 1.1: 20 colors picked as 14). Crop: Trace > Crop picture (Ctrl+R), then drag across the part of the picture to trace on the left side (the rest dimmed while dragging; Esc cancels); only that part is shown and traced, the SVG its size, and Auto settings for new pictures picks the settings for it (a screenshot's toolbars made a drawing look like something else to Auto). Cropping again crops further; Trace > Remove crop (Ctrl+Shift+R) brings the whole picture back; a new picture starts uncropped. The status bar says the size cropped to. Command line: --crop X,Y,W,H. Background: Remove on a picture with transparent pixels on its edge takes them as the background (a cropped part of a transparent picture, mostly covered by a dark shape at its edge, lost the shape as "the color around the edge" and had its transparent part come out white; the engine now keeps the given picture's alpha for this, kz_trace's thread). Tests: tests/test_crop.py (the dragged rectangle in picture pixels at any zoom, crop within a crop, remove, a new picture, the command line), crop steps in the app battery. README: "Compared with Inkscape" (accuracy, time and size on seven pictures against Inkscape's own Trace Bitmap code, whose tracing is 1.4.4's, and what each does), instead of the short table against the code before the fixes. Also: the slop check (tools/slop_check.py, in the unit tests and the full checks: debugging left in, TODO notes, unused imports, functions nothing uses, stray files, buzzwords and filler, placeholders, British spellings, doubled words, and menu commands, links and versions the docs name that don't exist); its first run found a "TODO: insert short description here" from Inkscape's file headers and four unused imports. |
